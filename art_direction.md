# Art Direction — 双月のアルケイア
- Style: painterly stylized fantasy, soft cel-shading, storybook warmth.
- Palette: dusk indigo #1b1d3a, moon ivory #f4ead2, ember amber #ffb347, frost cyan #8fd8ff, verdict gold #fff1a0.
- Lighting: twin moons (cool) + warm rim light; light fog.
- Subjects: original swordsman, frost mage, axe brute, winged judge-boss; arena stone floor; twin-moon sky.
- UI: ink-on-parchment plates, brass hairlines, vertical serif labels. No glossy neon.
